Frequently asked questions
How does my referral link work?
Your link (shown on the Earnings tab, with a QR code) takes people to a simple account-creation page — email and password, no payment details. Their signup is attributed to you the moment the account is created, before they even install the app. Anyone who joins through it, or types your code at signup in the app, counts as yours.
When does my link start working?
The moment your space goes live. Before that, the link shows an "invite not active" message — you can still prepare posters and posts, and it switches on automatically at go-live.
What do members get when they join through me?
A free first week with everything unlocked — no payment details up front. After the week, they can continue at your partner rate of £10 a month, applied automatically. They never need to remember a code.
How do I earn?
You earn your agreed share (standard 50%) of what each of your referred members actually pays, every month they stay subscribed. The Earnings tab shows your funnel — on free trial, paying, and trial-ended — and your estimated monthly earnings.
How and when do I get paid?
Monthly, by bank transfer from VYVE. Add your bank details in the Payout details card on the Earnings tab — only VYVE's finance team can see them. Your payout history appears on the same tab.
What about bookings?
Members can book your 1:1 services from your space. You keep 100% of booking revenue minus payment processing fees. Set your services, availability and blocked dates on the Bookings tab.
What happens to content I upload?
Everything you publish — videos, posts, session content — goes through a quick VYVE review before members can see it. You'll see the status against each item on the My Content tab. Therapy-related services also need clinical approval before they're bookable.
Can I see who my members are?
No — member privacy comes first, always. You see your numbers (trial, paying, lapsed), never identities. When you want to reach lapsed members, use the nudge button on the Earnings tab: we email them on your behalf, letting them know you asked us to check in.
What does "send a nudge" actually send?
A friendly email from VYVE to your lapsed members — "you joined through {your name}, and they asked us to check in" — with a link to pick up where they left off at your rate. One nudge batch per day, and each member is nudged at most once a month, so nobody gets spammed.
Something else?
Message us below — it goes straight to the VYVE team and we'll reply to your email. For anything urgent: team@vyvehealth.co.uk.
