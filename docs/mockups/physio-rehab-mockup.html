What's different from a PDF or a web link

  • The Rehab tab only exists while there's an active plan — it appears when the physio sends one and goes when the plan ends.
  • Each exercise is done or skipped, with a thumbs up/down; the sliders come once a day and only for what the physio ticked.
  • Push reminders at the prescribed times; videos cached for offline; the existing player, streaks and check-ins are reused.
  • "Ask Phil" lands in the coach thread that already exists in the app.
  • Every string here is a placeholder until Lewis signs it; the slider wording is RMP's for reference until Phil replaces it.
