  • Frequency:3 posts/week

Content Quality Standards
Lewis's voice, tone guidelines, and anti-repetition rules to maintain quality as the machine scales
Lewis's Voice Checklist
  • • Authentic and personal (use "I" / "we")
  • • Data-driven but not jargon-heavy
  • • Provocative without being controversial
  • • Action-oriented with clear takeaways
  • • Humble about what we don't know
  • • Curious and willing to learn publicly
  • • Generous with credit to sources and ideas
Tone by Platform
  • • LinkedIn: Professional authority, substantive
  • • Instagram: Visual inspiration, casual, relatable
  • • Facebook: Community focus, accessible, inclusive
  • • Never: Salesy, overly promotional, clickbait
  • • Always: Clear, honest, valuable, on-brand
Anti-Repetition Rules
  • • No topic appears 2× in same week
  • • Categories rotate (no 2 thought leadership in a row)
  • • Different angle/insight each topic instance
  • • Track previous 4 weeks for repetition patterns
  • • Vary data sources week to week
  • • 3-pillar balance: 40% mental / 40% physical / 20% social
Editorial Standards
  • • Every post cites source or provides attribution
  • • Proofread for tone consistency before publishing
  • • Images match brand aesthetic (colours, quality, style)
  • • Hashtags are branded and platform-appropriate
  • • Links work and point to relevant destination
  • • Engagement questions ask something interesting (not just "thoughts?")
